What happens on a Friday

You'll play from 8 to 10am, mixed in with other players so the games stay close whatever your level. Fancy some pointers? Our coach is on court the whole time. Never picked up a racket before? Half the group hadn't either.

After, we head to the café for breakfast and a proper catch up. That's where most of the actual networking happens, over coffee, not over a spreadsheet.

Founders, freelancers, tradespeople, agency owners. If you run or work in a Bristol business and fancy starting your Friday differently, come and play.

Questions first-timers ask

Something else? Head to the contact page and it'll go to the right person.

Do I need to be good at padel?

No. You're mixed in with other players so the games stay close whatever your level, and a coach is on court the whole time. Never picked up a racket? Half the group hadn't either.

Do I need to bring a racket?

No, rackets are provided.

What does it cost?

There's no membership fee. Sessions are £11, breakfast included, and your first one is free.

Who is it for?

Founders, freelancers, tradespeople and agency owners. If you run or work in a Bristol business and fancy starting your Friday differently, come and play.

When and where is it?

Every Friday, 8-10am, at Rocket Padel Bristol, St Anne's Rd, Brislington, BS4 4EB. Breakfast at the café follows.

How do I join?

Fill in the form below. James reviews every request personally and adds you to the WhatsApp group, so expect a reply before the week's out.

Is it a one-off or every week?

Every week. It's a standing group, not a subscription: once you're in the WhatsApp group, turn up whenever you can.
