About the group

The people behind your sessions

Business Padel Networking is run by three organisers and two coaches, all giving up their Friday morning to get Bristol's business community on court together.

James

Group Organiser

James set up Business Padel Networking to combine two things he's into: padel, and Bristol's business community. He reviews every request to join personally and keeps the WhatsApp group running week to week, so if you've messaged asking to join, he's the one reading it.

Outside of padel, James runs 299 Lighting, a Bristol-based commercial lighting design and supply company. 299 Lighting also supports the lighting on court every Friday.

Visit 299lighting.co.uk →

Yinka

Group Organiser

Yinka helps run the Friday sessions alongside James, from pairing players up to keeping the morning on schedule.

He also runs Courts and Connections, a Bristol-based corporate padel events company, creating team-building tournaments, client events and corporate leagues for businesses across the UK.

Visit courtsandconnections.com → LinkedIn →
B

Beth

Session Support, 299 Lighting

Beth helps run the Friday sessions alongside James and Yinka, and is part of the team at 299 Lighting.

Coached by Gabby and Will

Whatever your level, there's a coach on court every Friday to help you improve. Get in touch directly if you'd like extra sessions outside the group.

Gabby

Head Coach, Rocket Padel Bristol

An LTA Level 2 Padel Instructor, Gabby coaches players of every level through the fundamentals, from first serve to match play, with an eye for what each pair needs to work on that week.

Email Gabby @padelbygabriela →
W

Will Edlin

Individual & Group Coaching

Runs individual and group coaching on court every Friday through his business Elite Padel Coach, and is available to book directly for extra 1:1 or group sessions outside the usual Friday slot.
