# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Bristol business owners, freelancers, and professionals who want to combine fitness with networking. Likely 25-55, time-poor, already comfortable spending on wellness/social memberships. They discover this via word of mouth or a WhatsApp share and land on this page to decide in under 60 seconds whether to request a spot. All abilities welcome, so the page must not read as "sporty elite only."

## Product Purpose

A single landing page for the Bristol Business Padel Networking Group: a weekly Friday 8-10am padel + business networking session at Rocket Padel Bristol, run by James and Yinka. The page explains what happens, sells the vibe, and converts visitors into a WhatsApp group request via a short form (name, email/phone, business, padel level) that James reviews before adding people. Success = qualified business people requesting to join.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, premium-casual, credible. Feels like a members' sports club crossed with a founders' breakfast, not a corporate networking event and not a gym flyer. Confidence and momentum over corporate polish.

## Anti-references

Not generic SaaS (no cream backgrounds, no eyebrow labels, no hero-metric stat blocks). Not a stock-photo gym website. Not a stiff "Chamber of Commerce" networking page with a suit-and-tie feel — this is padel first, business second.

## Design Principles

- Padel-court energy: bold, kinetic, court-green + electric accent carrying real visual weight, not a token brand chip.
- One page, one decision: every section pushes toward requesting to join — no wandering nav, no filler sections.
- All-abilities welcome must be felt, not just stated — avoid imagery/copy that reads "pros only."
- Speed and clarity over ceremony — a visitor should grasp what/when/where/how in one hero scroll.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` throughout (crossfade fallback for all scroll/entrance animation). Maintain WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text) against the dark court-green/near-black palette — verified, not assumed, given the dark theme.
